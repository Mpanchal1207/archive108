ARCHIVE108 — DARK / EDGY STOREFRONT PROTOTYPE

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. Internet is only needed for the optional Google Fonts; the rest is local.

HOW TO ADD YOUR DESIGNS
- On the homepage, click the large hero placeholder to upload a hero image.
- Click a product image placeholder or its UPLOAD IMAGE label to upload an image for that design.
- Use UPLOAD PRODUCT IMAGES to upload up to five product images in order.
- These uploads are local browser previews only; they do not permanently save to the website files.
- To publish your images permanently, place image files in assets/ and update the image paths in index.html, or ask a developer to connect an image manager.

CURRENT PROTOTYPE FEATURES
- Responsive dark editorial streetwear layout.
- Five blank product slots.
- Local image previews for your own designs.
- Working demo shopping bag with quantities and subtotal.
- Checkout is intentionally disabled; no payments, fulfillment, account system, or newsletter backend is connected.

CUSTOMIZATION
- Colors, spacing, and typography are in style.css.
- Product names and demo prices are in script.js.
- Replace placeholder labels and prices before launch.
